Recording works again.

In the last version the pad could not reach the microphone at all. You tapped it, nothing was heard, and it said no microphone was available. The cause was a permission missing from the app itself, not anything on your phone, and it is fixed: hum a melody or a beat and it becomes a layer.

If you have ever refused the microphone, the pad now offers a way straight to the settings page where you can turn it back on.
