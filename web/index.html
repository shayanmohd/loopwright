Loopwright

The song in your head, out loud.

Hum a melody and it comes back as a felt piano. Say boots and cats and it comes back as drums. Stack the loops, drop layers in and out while it plays, and you have made something.

  • You need no theory and no instrument. Your voice is the input.
  • Headphones help a lot. Without them the microphone hears the loop as well as you.
  • Everything stays on this phone. Nothing is uploaded, because the app cannot reach the internet at all.
